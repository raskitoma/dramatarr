A wide episode gap is the interesting case. It is either one show cut differently by two platforms, or two different shows with a generic name. Compare the synopses before merging. Keep separate is permanent: the pair is never proposed again, and a merge you regret can still be undone, but a wrong merge shows viewers the wrong story in the meantime.
